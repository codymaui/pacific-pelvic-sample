Who it’s for

Personalized support from the comfort of home.

Not on Maui? Have a busy schedule? Or feel like you’ve tried traditional pelvic floor therapy but are still missing pieces of the puzzle?

Our online pelvic health coaching provides personalized education, movement guidance, and support to help you better understand your symptoms and work toward your goals.

In-depth initial consultation

We take time to understand your history, symptoms, lifestyle, and goals to identify areas where you may benefit from additional support.

Personalized movement & exercise programming

Receive guidance tailored to your body, whether you’re working toward improved pelvic health, returning to exercise, or building strength and confidence.

Whole-body approach

We look at the relationship between pelvic health, movement, strength, daily habits, and lifestyle rather than focusing on symptoms alone.

Ongoing guidance & support

Get individualized recommendations and adjustments as you progress toward your goals.

Our session will be

Personalized. Targeted. Integrative.

Personalized

Your unique needs and goals are at the forefront of our treatment approach. We will help tailor a specialized plan to address your specific concerns, ensuring that you receive the highest level of care and support on your journey to pelvic health.

Targeted

Through a comprehensive assessment, we will create a targeted plan designed to provide you with the precise care necessary to meet your goals and move into your future, worry free.

Integrative

Meeting your goals requires a combination of traditional physical therapy techniques, holistic practices, and personalized coaching to create a well rounded treatment plan. We take the physical and emotional aspects of healing and aim to provide a holistic experience that considers the individual as a whole.
