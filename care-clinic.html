What to expect

Care designed for lasting improvements.

Our pelvic floor physical therapy sessions are designed to help you understand your body, address the underlying factors contributing to your symptoms, and work toward lasting improvements.

Comprehensive evaluation

We take the time to understand your history, symptoms, lifestyle, and goals through a detailed, whole-body assessment.

Finding your WHY

We look beyond the pelvic floor to identify contributing factors, including muscle function, joint mobility, movement patterns, strength, and daily habits.

Individualized treatment

Your care may include hands-on manual therapy, targeted exercise, movement retraining, and education tailored to your body’s needs.

A plan for long-term success

You’ll leave your initial evaluation with a clearer understanding of your symptoms, practical recommendations, and a plan to help you reach your goals.

Our session will be

Personalized. Targeted. Integrative.

Personalized

Your unique needs and goals are at the forefront of our treatment approach. We will help tailor a specialized plan to address your specific concerns, ensuring that you receive the highest level of care and support on your journey to pelvic health.

Targeted

Through a comprehensive assessment, we will create a targeted plan designed to provide you with the precise care necessary to meet your goals and move into your future, worry free.

Integrative

Meeting your goals requires a combination of traditional physical therapy techniques, holistic practices, and personalized coaching to create a well rounded treatment plan. We take the physical and emotional aspects of healing and aim to provide a holistic experience that considers the individual as a whole.

First session

In your first session, we strive to provide you with relief, empowerment, and confidence to get you through this, feeling stronger and trusting in your body’s abilities.
